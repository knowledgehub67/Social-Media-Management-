What Is Social Media Management?

Social media management is the process of managing a business's presence on social platforms. It can include developing a content strategy, preparing posts and visual content, maintaining profiles, responding to audiences, monitoring activity and reviewing performance.

The right approach depends on the business, its audience, the platforms it uses and its communication goals. Consistent planning can also help keep brand information and visual communication aligned across social channels.
